## 📌 Flagship Systems & Engineering

### 🩸 [UNITE](https://portfolio.pkov.online) — *Co-Founder & Lead Developer* `(2025–Present)`
Regional health-tech startup in clinical collaboration with **Bicol Medical Center (BMC)**.
- Real-time inventory tracking for PRBC, FFP, platelets, and cryoprecipitate across 8 blood groups with shelf-life degradation counters.
- Strict institutional tenant isolation via engine-level **PostgreSQL Row-Level Security (RLS)** across multi-hospital schemas.
- Automated donor mobilization reducing dispatch latency from multi-hour phone trees to **under 45 seconds**.
- Offline-tolerant **React Native** field app for volunteer coordinators running community blood drives.

### 🏥 ap-silang — *Lead Full-Stack Developer* `(2025–2026)`
Capstone flagship: municipal maternal healthcare EMR PWA for rural health units and mountain barangays.
- Offline-first EMR PWA with a **Dexie.js (IndexedDB)** append-only outbox journal achieving deterministic local write latency **under 4 ms**.
- Priority-weighted sync reconciliation queue (`triage > prescriptions > vitals`) to resolve disconnected-state conflicts without server-roundtrip freezes.
- In-browser **Targeted Early Warning Score (TEWS)** triage CDSS computing maternal risk scores in **under 1 ms** offline.
- PIN-gated referral handover gateways compliant with Philippine **DOH 28-year** maternal record retention standards.

### ⚖️ RTC Clearance Express — *Lead Full-Stack Developer* `(2026)`
Civic tech prototype for the **Regional Trial Court, Hall of Justice, Iriga City**.
- Digitized counter filing with a 4-step citizen self-registration portal, reducing lobby intake from hours to **~3 minutes**.
- Real-time Clerk Workstation with QR verification and a live `8.5 × 13-inch` official court certificate preview & print engine.
